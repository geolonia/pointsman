Demos

Try Pointsman in the browser. Each demo runs live on public data, with nothing to install. The reports in them are made up.

Closed roads in heavy rain

Residents report closed roads. Pointsman reads each report, checks the place on public maps (flood zones, evacuation sites, the way around) and decides in seconds: put it on the map, ask a person, or treat it as urgent.
