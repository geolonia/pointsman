Pointsman is an open-source decision service. Your systems send it a
*case*, something to decide, for example a report of a closed road or a new
pull request. Pointsman asks
fixed questions, applies rules that people wrote, and answers with an action.
When it is not sure, a person checks the case.

New here? Read [The decision model](explanation/decision-model.md) first,
then [Profiles at a glance](reference/profiles.md).
